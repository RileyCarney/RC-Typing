## Compliance Notes
- All code excerpts are permissively licensed (MIT, Apache-2.0).
- Encyclopedic excerpts adhere to CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.
- Historical texts and US government publications are in the Public Domain.
- Corpus text files are bundled offline in the standalone container and do not require external network requests during typing sessions.
